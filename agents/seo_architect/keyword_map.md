## Keyword Guardrails

- No keyword stuffing — max 1-2 uses of primary keyword per page
- Each page targets a unique primary intent — no two pages compete for the same keyword
- City modifiers added naturally to content, not forced
- Long-tail lyrics pages are the volume play — keep them clean and complete
