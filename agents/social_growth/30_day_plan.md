# Wavyarnold — 30-Day Short-Form Content Plan

**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
**Goal:** Drive followers → streams → email/SMS list sign-ups
**Brand rule:** Hook in first 1–2 seconds. No forced trends. No cringe.

---

## Posting Cadence

| Platform | Frequency | Best Days |
|----------|-----------|-----------|
| TikTok | 5–7x/week | Tue, Thu, Sat, Sun |
| Instagram Reels | 4–5x/week | Mon, Wed, Fri, Sat |
| YouTube Shorts | 2–3x/week | Wed, Fri |

---

## Content Mix (by type)

| Type | % of Posts | Description |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| Music clips | 40% | 15–30 sec snippets with hook in first 2 sec |
| Process / behind the scenes | 20% | Studio, writing, recording moments |
| Personal / voice-to-camera | 20% | Talking directly to camera — no script feel |
| Lyric / text overlay | 10% | Punchy lyric over beat or ambient visual |
| Reaction / storytelling | 10% | Short form narrative about a song or moment |

## Hook Library (Openers to Rotate)

- "This one started as a voice memo at 2am."
- "I almost didn't drop this."
- "Here's the part of making music nobody films."
- "This line means something specific."
- "Made in Newark. Here's the version before the polish."
- "I need you to hear the first 20 seconds of this."
- "The story behind this song is actually about [one specific thing]."
- "Most artists don't show this. I'm showing it anyway."
