## Content Guardrails

- Every post ends with one CTA: Join the list, stream a track, or view upcoming shows
- Never write a post that could apply to any artist — be specific to Wavyarnold's experience
- Blog posts: minimum 600 words, target 900–1200 for SEO value
- Lyrics pages: full lyrics + 2–3 sentence "About This Song" blurb + streaming links
