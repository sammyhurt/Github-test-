## 1. Behind-the-Song Blog Post Template

```markdown
# Behind "[Song Title]" — [Short Teaser Line]

*By Wavyarnold | [Date] | [X] min read*

---

[Opening paragraph — drop the reader into a specific moment, image, or feeling.
No "I'm excited to share this song." Start in the middle of something real.]

---

## Where This One Came From

[2–3 paragraphs. Be specific. What was happening in life? What were you trying to figure out?
Don't explain the song — tell the story behind it.]

## The Line That Matters

> "[Pull a specific lyric that anchors the post]"

[1–2 paragraphs explaining this line's context — not the whole song, just this one piece.]

## What I Hope You Hear

[1 paragraph. What does this song do for the listener? What feeling are you hoping lands?
Don't be preachy. Be honest.]

---

Stream "[Song Title]" now → [Link]

And if you want more like this — the story behind the music, not just the music —
[join the list](/join). New drops, real talk, nothing you didn't sign up for.
```

```markdown
FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

**WAVYARNOLD RELEASES "[SONG/PROJECT TITLE]"**
*Newark, NJ Artist Delivers [One-Phrase Honest Description] on New [Single/EP/Album]*

NEWARK, NJ — [Date] — Newark-based Hip-Hop and Alternative artist Wavyarnold has released
"[Title]," a new [single/EP/project] available now on all major streaming platforms.

"[Direct quote from Wavyarnold about the release — specific, in his voice, not a PR quote]"

[Paragraph 2 — context on the release. What does it sound like? What is it about?
Be factual and specific. No "groundbreaking," no "genre-defying."]

[Paragraph 3 — optional: context on the artist, upcoming shows, or what's next.]

"[Title]" is available on Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, and YouTube.

For press inquiries and interview requests: [EMAIL]
For booking: [EMAIL or CONTACT PAGE]

**About Wavyarnold**
[SHORT BIO — use the 50-75 word version from bios.md]

**Links**
```

## Template Guardrails

- Never start a blog post with "I'm excited to share..."
- Never use "groundbreaking," "genre-defying," or "innovative sound" in press releases
- Every piece of content links to at least one other page on the site
- Every piece ends with one CTA — don't stack multiple asks
