## Visual Direction Notes

- **Colors:** Deep muted tones — midnight blue, iron gray, off-white, and one accent (warm amber or saturated red). No neon. No candy palettes.
- **Typography:** Mix of a clean sans-serif (body/info) with a weathered or hand-touched display font (name/titles). Avoid generic rap poster fonts.
- **Imagery:** Film grain. Natural light or intentional darkness. Real environments over staged shoots. Newark backdrops where possible.
- **Feel:** Like a well-worn journal with something real inside — not a glossy ad, not a lo-fi aesthetic just for aesthetic's sake.
