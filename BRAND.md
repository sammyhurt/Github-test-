# Subletly Brand Guidelines

## Brand Identity

**Mission:** Make short-term housing frictionless for travel nurses and the hosts who serve them.

**Voice:** Warm, confident, and empowering — never corporate, never cold.

**Tagline:** *"Your home, wherever work takes you."*

## Logo Usage

- Minimum size: 80px wide
- Clear-space: 1× logo-height on all sides
- On dark backgrounds: white wordmark only
- On light backgrounds: `#5221C3` wordmark only
- Never stretch, recolor, or add drop shadows

---

## Photography & Imagery

- **Real spaces:** bright, airy interiors with natural light
- **People:** diverse travel nurses in scrubs + casual wear
- **Mood:** purposeful calm — someone who is settled, not stressed
- Avoid stock-photo clichés (fake smiling, generic handshakes)

---

## Voice & Tone

### Do
- Speak directly to the travel nurse or host ("You deserve...")
- Use active verbs ("Rescue your booking", "Claim your spot")
- Acknowledge the stress of the job before offering relief
- Short sentences. One idea at a time.

### Don't
- Use jargon ("synergistic housing solutions")
- Overpromise or use superlatives without proof
- Speak condescendingly about hosts or nurses
- Use passive voice in CTAs

## Accessibility

- Minimum contrast ratio: 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text
- All videos: closed captions required
- Alt text: descriptive, not keyword-stuffed
- Never convey meaning through color alone
