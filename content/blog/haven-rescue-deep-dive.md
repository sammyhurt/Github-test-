## What Is Haven Rescue?

Haven Rescue is Subletly's emergency housing protocol — a one-tap feature that activates the Haven Engine the moment your booking falls through.

Here's how it works in practice:

**Step 1: Trigger is detected.**
Either you tap the "Trigger Haven Rescue" button in the app, or — if you're within 72 hours of check-in — the system detects the cancellation automatically and activates on your behalf.

**Step 2: The Haven Engine activates.**
Our real-time matching algorithm begins scanning our network of verified nurse-friendly hosts. It filters by:
- Commute radius to your assigned hospital (default: 30 minutes)
- Your budget range (pulled from your original booking parameters)
- Move-in availability (within your required window)
- Haven Score (our composite host quality metric — more on this below)

**Step 3: A curated shortlist, not a spam wall.**
Within 45 minutes, you receive a shortlist of 3–5 top-matched hosts. Not 200 listings to scroll through. Three to five vetted options, ranked by compatibility.

**Step 4: You confirm. You move in.**
Message your top match directly inside the Subletly app. Hosts in Haven Rescue mode have a **60-minute response SLA** — if they don't respond, their account is paused and the next host on your list is notified automatically.

**Step 5: Crisis resolved.**
Haven Rescue has a **94% same-day resolution rate.** For the remaining 6%, Subletly's 24/7 dedicated nurse support team steps in directly — with hotel reimbursement coverage and direct host outreach.

## The Haven Engine: How the Matching Works

The Haven Engine is the algorithm under the hood of every Haven Rescue. Understanding it helps you understand why the matches are good — not just fast.

### Haven Score

Every host on Subletly has a Haven Score (0–5.0). It's not the same as a star rating. Here's what goes into it:

| Factor | Weight | Description |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| Nurse Reviews | 40% | Reviews left specifically by verified travel nurses (2× the algorithmic weight vs. general reviews) |
| Response Rate | 20% | Percentage of messages answered within 4 hours over the last 90 days |
| Cancellation History | 20% | Weighted by proximity-to-arrival (a day-before cancel costs more than a 30-day cancel) |
| Check-In Flexibility | 10% | How often host accommodates non-standard check-in times (common for nurses with shift schedules) |
| Repeat Nurse Bookings | 10% | How often the same nurse books with the same host (a proxy for satisfaction) |

The key difference: **nurse reviews count twice.**

We made this change because travel nurses have a different standard of "good host" than leisure travelers. A host who is lovely for a vacation couple might be a nightmare for a nurse working 7 PM–7 AM shifts. Nurse reviewers know what to look for. We amplify that signal.

### Real-Time Filtering

During a Haven Rescue event, the Engine runs a live filter that goes beyond static profile data:

- **Current availability:** Cross-referenced with the host's live calendar, not their self-reported availability.
- **Recent activity:** Has this host logged in within the last 7 days? Dormant accounts are filtered out.
- **Active rescue capacity:** If a host is already accommodating another Haven Rescue guest, they're deprioritized to prevent overstretching.

### Speed vs. Quality Trade-Off

We made a deliberate choice: **quality over volume.**

The temptation in a rescue scenario is to flood the nurse with options. More options = more stress in a high-stakes moment. Our research showed that nurses in crisis made worse decisions when presented with more than 7 options. We cap the shortlist at 5. We optimize for decision confidence, not option count.

## Who Builds These Networks?

A matching algorithm is only as good as the host network it draws from.

Subletly's verified host network is curated, not scraped. Here's what it takes to become a Nurse-Verified host on Subletly:

1. **Profile verification:** Government ID, property ownership or management agreement, and a video walkthrough reviewed by our team.
2. **Nurse-readiness audit:** We check for fast WiFi, blackout curtains, a quiet space for sleeping during the day (critical for night-shift nurses), and flexible check-in windows.
3. **Haven Pledge:** Every verified host signs the Haven Pledge — committing to the 60-minute Haven Rescue response SLA, and agreeing to the 72-hour post check-in protection window.
4. **Ongoing monitoring:** Haven Scores are recalculated weekly. Hosts who drop below 3.5 are reviewed. Those who cancel on Haven Rescue guests within 30 days face immediate suspension.

The result: a network of hosts who aren't just open to hosting nurses — they've specifically prepared for it.
