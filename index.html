FAQ

Common questions.

When does Subletly launch in Rochester?

We're onboarding founding hosts now. The first verified units go live for booking in [TBD month]. Founding waitlist members get first access — and 0% tenant fee on first stays at founding-host units.

How is Subletly different from Furnished Finder?

Three things FF doesn't do: (1) every host completes ID verification + Right-to-Occupy proof before listing; (2) deposits go to Stripe escrow, not direct to the host; (3) Haven safety net covers a 48-hour hotel + full refund if move-in fails. FF is a directory; we're a trust layer.

Do I need to be self-placing to use Subletly?

For the founding cohort, yes — Subletly is for nurses taking the housing stipend rather than agency-provided housing. We may add agency partnerships in v2.

What does the booking fee cover?

4% of the booking total. For founding-cohort tenants at founding-host units, the fee is 0% on your first stay. Standard fee covers ID verification, escrow infrastructure, attorney-reviewed lease, and Haven move-in protection.

What if I arrive and the apartment isn't as listed?

That's exactly what Haven is for. We cover a hotel up to 48 hours while we resolve the issue. If we can't, you get a full refund — including the booking fee.

How do you verify hosts?

Stripe Identity for ID + biometric match. Right-to-Occupy via redacted lease, property tax record, or management consent. Photo + walkthrough verification of every unit before it goes live.

What happens to my deposit?

Held in Stripe Connect escrow. Released to the host only when you confirm move-in. If you're not satisfied within the move-in window, deposit returns to you — full amount, including platform fee.

How does payment timing work?

You pay 50% of your booking when you book, and the remaining 50% two business days before move-in. Stripe processes everything; we hold the funds in escrow until you confirm move-in.
