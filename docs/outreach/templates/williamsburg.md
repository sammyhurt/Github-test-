## Platform Do/Don't Notes

### Facebook Groups
- **Do:** Keep tone polished but not stiff — Williamsburg hosts often have some business savvy
- **Do:** Mention tenant vetting early — that's the trust signal that resonates here
- **Don't:** Lead with price advice (they usually know their market)
- **Don't:** Use overly casual slang — this audience responds to competence, not friendliness alone

### Craigslist
- **Do:** Subject line: "Mid-term tenant inquiry — [street/neighborhood]"
- **Do:** Reference your professional renter network specifically
- **Don't:** Generic intros — Williamsburg landlords get a lot of cold outreach

### Reddit
- **Do:** Engage substantively with questions — this audience is skeptical of anything promotional
- **Do:** Acknowledge you're with Subletly upfront if asked
- **Don't:** Oversell — let the advice do the work
- **Don't:** Post in threads where OP has already found a tenant (check for "FOUND" edits)
