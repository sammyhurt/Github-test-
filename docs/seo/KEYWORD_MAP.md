## Keyword Strategy Overview

### Subletly's Keyword Advantage

Sublet marketplaces occupy a specific niche in real estate search:
- Renters searching for sublets have **high urgency** (students, job relocators, traveling workers)
- Landlords/Craigslist dominate generic "apartments" terms — **avoid those**
- "Sublet" + "short-term rental" + "furnished apartment" terms are **underserved** by big platforms
- University city keywords have **seasonal demand spikes** (Aug–Sep move-in, Jan semester start)

### Keyword Tiers

| Tier | Example | Intent | Page type | Volume | KD |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 — City transactional | `sublets in chicago` | Renter ready to search | City hub | High | Med |
| T2 — Neighborhood transactional | `sublets lincoln park chicago` | High-intent renter | Neighborhood hub | Med | Low |
| T3 — National head terms | `sublet apartments` | Mixed | Homepage | Very high | Very high |
| T4 — Informational | `how to sublet an apartment` | Lister/renter research | Blog/FAQ | Med | Low |
| T5 — Comparison | `sublet vs short-term rental` | Research | Blog | Low | Low |
| T6 — University | `sublets near university of chicago` | Student renter | Neighborhood hub | Med | Very low |

**Priority order:** T2 > T1 > T6 > T4 > T5 > T3

T3 requires too much DA to compete short-term. T2 and T6 have low KD and high conversion intent.

## Keywords to Avoid (Too Competitive)

Do not target these with SEO effort until DA > 40:

| Keyword | Why to Avoid |
|---|---|
| `apartments for rent [city]` | Dominated by Zillow, Apartments.com, Craigslist |
| `short term rentals [city]` | Airbnb, VRBO dominate |
| `furnished apartments [city]` | Furnished Finder, Airbnb dominate |
| `rooms for rent [city]` | Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace |
| `monthly rentals [city]` | Very competitive, low sublet specificity |

## Internal Linking Keyword Anchor Text Rules

- **Do:** Use keyword-rich anchors: "sublets in Lincoln Park" → `/sublets/chicago/lincoln-park`
- **Do:** Vary anchor text naturally (don't repeat exact same anchor on every page)
- **Don't:** Generic anchors: "click here", "learn more", "read this"
- **Don't:** Exact-match on every link (over-optimization signal)

**Anchor text ratio target:** 40% exact/phrase match, 40% partial match, 20% branded/generic
